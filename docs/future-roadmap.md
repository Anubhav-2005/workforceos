# Future roadmap

The MVP proves the product loop: assign work, let specialized AI employees collaborate, stop for human judgment, and report the outcome. The next phases turn that loop into a durable multi-tenant product.

## Phase 1 — Durable foundation

- Authentication and workspace membership
- Role-based access control
- Database-backed tasks, candidates, approvals, and workflow runs
- Encrypted file storage with configurable retention
- Shared rate limiting
- Structured audit events

## Phase 2 — Real execution

- Durable background jobs and retries
- Workflow versioning
- Conditional branches and reusable node templates
- Scheduled and event-triggered workflows
- Idempotent external actions
- Approval reminders and escalation policies

## Phase 3 — Business integrations

- ATS and HRIS connectors for recruiting
- CRM and email connectors for sales
- Helpdesk and knowledge-base connectors for support
- Calendar scheduling
- Slack and Microsoft Teams approval actions

## Phase 4 — Intelligence and governance

- Role-specific model routing
- Prompt and model evaluation suites
- Quality, latency, and cost dashboards
- Policy controls by action risk
- Evidence and citation capture
- Configurable data residency and retention

## Phase 5 — Marketplace

- Custom AI employee builder
- Reusable workforce templates
- Partner-built connectors and skills
- Domain packs for recruiting, sales, support, finance, and operations

## North-star measures

- work completed without manual coordination;
- hours reclaimed per active workspace;
- approval turnaround time;
- successful workflow completion rate;
- cost per completed business outcome;
- human override and error rates.
